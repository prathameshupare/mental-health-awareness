UNDERSTANDING MENTAL HEALTH

What is mental health?

Mental health refers to our emotional, psychological and social wellbeing. It can influence how we think, feel, behave, handle stress and connect with others.

Just like physical health, mental health can change throughout life. Everyone can experience difficult emotions or periods of stress, and asking for support is a normal part of taking care of ourselves.

“

Taking care of your mental health is not a sign of weakness. It is part of taking care of yourself.

A HOLISTIC APPROACH

Mental wellbeing has many dimensions

💭

Emotional

Understanding emotions and learning healthy ways to respond to difficult situations.

🧩

Psychological

Developing healthy thinking patterns, resilience and ways of coping with everyday challenges.

🤝

Social

Building positive relationships and maintaining meaningful connections with other people.

BREAKING THE STIGMA

Myths vs facts

MYTH

"Talking about mental health makes things worse."

FACT

Open and supportive conversations can make it easier for people to seek appropriate help.

MYTH

"Asking for help means you are weak."

FACT

Seeking support is a normal part of taking care of your wellbeing.

MYTH

"Mental health problems are always obvious."

FACT

People may experience difficulties that are not immediately visible to others.

A BIGGER PICTURE

Mental health is part of everyday health

Wellbeing can be influenced by sleep, relationships, study or work pressure, environment, physical health and access to support. It can change over time, and different people can respond differently to similar situations.
