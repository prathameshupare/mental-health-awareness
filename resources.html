COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological and social wellbeing. It affects how we think, feel, behave and interact with others.

Stress is a common human response to challenging situations. If stress becomes overwhelming or persistent, talking to someone you trust or a qualified professional may be helpful.

Consider seeking support when emotional or psychological difficulties persist, become difficult to manage or interfere with everyday life.

Listen without judgement, show empathy, stay connected and encourage them to reach out to appropriate support when needed.
