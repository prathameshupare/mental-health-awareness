KNOWLEDGE IS A FIRST STEP

Common mental health challenges

Mental health experiences can be different for everyone. Learning about common challenges can reduce stigma and encourage people to seek appropriate support.

01
🌧️

Stress

Stress can occur when we feel unable to cope with demands or pressures. Learning healthy coping strategies can help.

02
🌊

Anxiety

Anxiety can involve persistent worry, fear or nervousness. When it becomes difficult to manage, professional support may be helpful.

03
🌱

Low Mood

Periods of sadness or low mood can happen to anyone. If these feelings persist or interfere with daily life, talking to a professional can help.

04
🔥

Burnout

Long periods of excessive pressure can leave a person feeling emotionally and physically exhausted.

05
🏠

Loneliness

Feeling disconnected from others can affect emotional wellbeing. Meaningful social connections can provide valuable support.

06
📚

Academic Pressure

Students may experience pressure related to studies, exams, expectations and future plans.

PAY ATTENTION

Recognising when someone may need support

Changes in behaviour or mood can sometimes indicate that someone is going through a difficult period. One sign alone does not diagnose a mental health condition.

✓ Noticeable changes in mood
✓ Withdrawing from usual activities
✓ Difficulty concentrating
✓ Changes in sleep or daily routine
✓ Feeling overwhelmed for a long period
✓ Difficulty managing everyday responsibilities

NOTICE EARLY

When everyday stress starts affecting daily life

Everyone has difficult days. Consider reaching out when changes in mood, sleep, concentration, relationships or daily functioning persist or become difficult to manage.

Common experiences

  • Feeling overwhelmed by ongoing responsibilities
  • Persistent worry or difficulty switching off
  • Low motivation or loss of interest in usual activities
  • Changes in sleep, appetite or energy
  • Difficulty concentrating on studies or work

What can help

Start with a trusted person, a teacher, counsellor, healthcare professional or another appropriate support service. You do not need to wait until things feel unbearable before asking for help.
