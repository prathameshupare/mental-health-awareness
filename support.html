YOU DON'T HAVE TO HANDLE EVERYTHING ALONE

How to support someone

01

Listen

Give them your attention without immediately trying to solve the problem.

02

Show empathy

Let them know that their feelings matter and that they are not being judged.

03

Encourage help

Encourage them to speak with a trusted person or qualified mental health professional when appropriate.

04

Stay connected

A simple message or check-in can remind someone that they are not alone.

START THE CONVERSATION

Simple ways to be there for someone

1

Choose a calm moment

Talk somewhere reasonably private and comfortable.

2

Listen without rushing

Give them space to explain what they are experiencing.

3

Ask what would help

Offer practical support without assuming you know exactly what they need.

4

Encourage appropriate help

If difficulties are persistent or serious, encourage contact with a qualified professional or trusted support service.

PAY ATTENTION

Recognising when someone may need support

Changes in behaviour or mood can sometimes indicate that someone is going through a difficult period. One sign alone does not diagnose a mental health condition.

✓ Noticeable changes in mood
✓ Withdrawing from usual activities
✓ Difficulty concentrating
✓ Changes in sleep or daily routine
✓ Feeling overwhelmed for a long period
✓ Difficulty managing everyday responsibilities
