SMALL STEPS MATTER

Healthy habits for wellbeing

Self-care does not replace professional help, but healthy everyday habits can support overall wellbeing.

😴

Sleep

Maintain a consistent sleep routine and give your body enough time to rest.

🚶

Movement

Regular physical activity such as walking can support physical and mental wellbeing.

🥗

Nutrition

Regular, balanced meals and adequate hydration support overall health.

📵

Digital Breaks

Taking occasional breaks from screens can create space for rest and offline activities.

👥

Connection

Spend time with people you trust and maintain meaningful social connections.

🎨

Enjoyment

Make time for hobbies and activities that help you relax and recharge.

YOUR DAILY RESET

Healthy habits do not have to be complicated

Self-care is not about being productive all the time. It can mean protecting your sleep, taking breaks, moving your body comfortably, spending time with supportive people and making room for activities you enjoy.
